# Prospection templates — English

Read by `scripts/prospection/plume.ts`. Same four-paragraph structure as
`content/prospection-fr.md`, same rules: no price in a first message, one question,
the report does the selling.

**A note on who gets these.** Both published editions measure the **French** market.
A brand reaches this file only because its headquarters are outside France — Drunk
Elephant, Paula's Choice, Tata Harper are ranked in the French beauty barometer while
being American companies. The email is in English because that is the language of the
inbox; the ranking it describes is French, and the templates say so plainly rather
than glossing over it.

CAN-SPAM requires a physical postal address in every message sent to the US. It comes
from `PROSPECT_POSTAL_ADDRESS`, and the Contrôleur refuses to release a US-bound email
without it.
